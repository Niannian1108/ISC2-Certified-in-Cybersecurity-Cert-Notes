# The Confidentiality, Integrity and Avaliability (CIA) Triad
Confidentiality --> permitting authorised access to information, protecting from improper disclosure.  
Integrity --> Property of information, ensures completeness, accuracy, internal consistency, and usefulness for a stated purpose.  
Availability --> Accessible when needed.

# Authentication
## Confidentiality
Process of validation to prove rightful owner of identity. (Prove identity of the requestor)
Three common methods for authentication:
- Something you know [Passwords/passphrases]
- Something you have [Tokens, memory cards, smart cards]
- Something you are [Biometrics, measurable characteristics]

# Professional Code of Conduct
## ISC2 Code of Ethics Preamble
The purpose and intent of the ISC2 Code of Ethics
- Adhere to the highest ethical standards, for the safety and welfare of society and the common good, duty to principles and duty to each other.
- Strict adherence is a condition of certification.
## ISC2 Code of Ethics Canon
The important beliefs held in common by the members of ISC2. 
- Protect society, common good, necessary public trust and confidence, and the infrastructure.
- Act honourably, honestly, justly, responsibly, and legally.
- Provide diligent and competent service to principles.
- Advance and protect the profession.
