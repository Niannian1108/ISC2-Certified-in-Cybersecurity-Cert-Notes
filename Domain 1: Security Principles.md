# The Confidentiality, Integrity and Avaliability (CIA) Triad
Confidentiality --> permitting authorised access to information, protecting from improper disclosure.
Integrity --> Property of information, ensures completeness, accuracy, internal consistency, and usefulness for a stated purpose.
Availability --> Accessible when needed. 

# Privacy
Privacy --> Right to control the distrubution of information about oneself.

2016: European Union passed comprehensive legislation that addresses personla privacy, deeming it an individual human right.
